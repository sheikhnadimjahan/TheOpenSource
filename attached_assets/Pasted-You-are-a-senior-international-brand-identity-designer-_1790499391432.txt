You are a senior international brand identity designer and art director specializing in world-class news, editorial, publishing, and digital media brands.

Your task is to create the definitive visual identity and logo system for:

THEOPENSOURCE.NEWS

Brand name:
TheOpenSource.NEWS

The brand is intended to become a serious international digital news publication. The identity must communicate:
• global journalism
• openness and transparency
• credibility
• intelligence
• editorial authority
• modern digital publishing
• human interest
• technology and the open web
• beauty and sophistication
• timelessness

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT produce a generic technology logo.
Do NOT simply put a globe beside a wordmark.
Do NOT copy or imitate Reuters, BBC, CNN, The Guardian, The New York Times, National Geographic, AP, Bloomberg, or any other existing news brand.

Study the design principles behind internationally recognized editorial identities, but create a completely original identity.

DESIGN OBJECTIVE

Create a logo that can stand beside major international news organizations without looking amateur, local, generic, or AI-generated.

The logo must be beautiful at first sight, but beauty alone is not enough.

It must have:
1. instant recognition
2. strong silhouette
3. excellent typography
4. intelligent symbolism
5. sophisticated colour
6. excellent negative space
7. strong performance at very small sizes
8. strong performance in large mastheads
9. excellent black-and-white reproduction
10. excellent digital and mobile usability

TYPOGRAPHY

Typography is extremely important.

Do not use an ordinary default sans-serif.

Explore sophisticated editorial typography, especially:
• premium contemporary serif
• elegant high-contrast serif
• refined newspaper/editorial type
• custom or modified letterforms
• carefully paired serif + modern sans-serif

The typography should feel established, intelligent and international.

The words “TheOpenSource” must have a distinctive visual personality.

“.NEWS” should integrate naturally with the main wordmark rather than appearing like an afterthought.

Pay particular attention to:
• letter spacing
• kerning
• x-height
• stroke contrast
• proportions
• optical balance
• capitalization
• relationship between “The”, “OpenSource” and “.NEWS”

COLOUR

The previous concept was structurally strong but received only about 80% approval because the colour system was not sophisticated enough.

Develop a much stronger colour strategy.

Do not use too many colours.

Explore a restrained premium editorial palette based around:
• deep navy / intellectual blue
• refined cobalt or royal blue
• black / charcoal
• white
• one carefully controlled accent colour

Red may be explored only as a very limited editorial signal, not as the dominant colour.

The colours must work:
• on white
• on black
• in monochrome
• on a website
• on mobile
• as a favicon
• on social media
• in print

SYMBOL

Develop an original symbol that can stand independently from the wordmark.

The symbol may intelligently reference concepts such as:
• openness
• global communication
• information
• source
• connection
• discovery
• journalism
• the open web
• the world

But avoid clichés.

Do NOT automatically use:
• a standard globe
• microphone
• newspaper page
• camera
• RSS icon
• generic speech bubble
• generic network nodes
• AI robot
• satellite
• magnifying glass

If a globe is used, transform it into something distinctive and ownable rather than using a conventional globe illustration.

The symbol should ideally work as:
• favicon
• app icon
• social avatar
• website browser icon
• video watermark
• mobile application icon
• standalone brand mark

LOGO SYSTEM

Create a complete logo family:

A. Primary horizontal logo
B. Compact logo
C. Symbol-only mark
D. Wordmark-only version
E. Black version
F. White/reversed version
G. Monochrome version
H. Small-size/favicon version

WEBSITE APPLICATION

TheOpenSource.NEWS will be a digital-first publication.

Show how the identity would behave in a premium international news website.

The masthead must look excellent above:
• breaking news
• major headlines
• photography
• investigative journalism
• world news
• technology
• science
• culture
• human-interest stories

The logo must not overpower journalism.

It should make the website feel more trustworthy, beautiful and internationally established.

VISUAL DIRECTION

Think:
The Guardian-level editorial confidence
+
Reuters-level global recognition
+
BBC-level simplicity
+
National Geographic-level symbolic memorability

But create something entirely ORIGINAL.

Do not copy their logos, fonts, layouts or visual marks.

The final identity should feel:
• premium
• intelligent
• editorial
• global
• human
• contemporary
• timeless
• restrained
• memorable

It must NOT feel:
• cheap
• corporate-template-like
• gaming-related
• crypto-related
• generic SaaS
• overly futuristic
• cartoonish
• excessively colourful
• over-designed

DELIVERABLE

Do not stop after producing one attractive logo.

First explore several genuinely different design directions internally.

Then select the strongest direction based on:
• memorability
• typography
• symbolism
• scalability
• colour
• editorial credibility
• international appeal
• uniqueness

Produce the final recommended identity.

Show the logo clearly and professionally.

IMPORTANT FINAL STANDARD

Do not optimize for “pretty”.

Optimize for a logo that could realistically become a globally recognizable news-media brand over the next 10–20 years.

The final result should look deliberately designed by a world-class editorial branding studio, not generated from a generic logo template.

Brand spelling must be EXACTLY:

TheOpenSource.NEWS

No alternative spelling.
